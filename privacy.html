PeakCut Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

1. Introduction

PeakCut is an open-source, self-hosted video clipping application. This policy explains how information may be handled when you use PeakCut.

2. Information and Content

Depending on your setup and use, PeakCut may process video files, transcripts, prompts, generated clips, and application settings on your server.

3. AI Providers

If you configure a third-party AI provider, relevant data may be sent to that provider for processing. Its privacy policy and terms apply to that processing.

4. API Keys

You may provide API keys for external services. You are responsible for protecting these credentials and securing your server.

5. Data Storage and Security

PeakCut is self-hosted. Data storage, retention, access, and security depend on the server configuration and the person operating the installation.

6. Third-Party Services

PeakCut may connect to external services. We do not control their data practices, availability, or privacy policies.

7. Your Responsibility

You are responsible for ensuring that your use of PeakCut complies with applicable privacy and data protection laws.

8. Contact

For questions, visit the PeakCut GitHub repository.
